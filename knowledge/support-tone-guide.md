# Talking to upset customers

- Acknowledge impact in their words first ("Your dispatchers can't log in during peak — that's serious.").
- Say what you **know**, what you're **doing**, and **when** they'll hear next. Never guess an ETA.
- Own it when it's our fault. Don't blame the customer's IdP until it's confirmed.
- Close every call with explicit commitments and an owner.
