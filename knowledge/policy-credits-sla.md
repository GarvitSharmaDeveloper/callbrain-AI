# Credits & SLA Policy

- Enterprise SLA: 99.9% monthly availability for login and dispatch features.
- Support agents may offer an SLA credit of **up to 10% of the monthly fee** when an Orbit-caused outage breaches SLA.
- **Only one agent-approved credit per customer per 90 days.** A second credit within 90 days requires **Support Manager approval** (Sam Ortiz).
- Credits above 10% of the monthly fee require VP Finance approval (Tara Brooks).
- Never offer a credit before the incident is confirmed as Orbit-caused.
