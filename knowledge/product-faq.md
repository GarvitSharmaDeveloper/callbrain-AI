# Product FAQ

- **Data export:** Reports → Export. Large exports (>100k rows) are emailed as a link within 15 minutes.
- **Label printers:** supported Zebra ZD/ZT series; reinstall driver v6.2 for "printer offline".
- **User limits:** Enterprise plans have unlimited dispatcher seats.
- **Status page:** status.orbit.io (incidents are posted within 15 minutes of declaration).
