# Refund Policy

§1 Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

§2 **Duplicate charges** caused by an Orbit system error (e.g. a payment retry) are refunded **in full, with no approval required**, regardless of amount. Billing Ops processes them within **3 business days**. Support may promise the customer: "You'll see the refund within 3 business days; your bank may take 5–10 days to display it."

§3 Refunds for service dissatisfaction are not offered; use SLA credits instead (see Credits & SLA Policy).

§4 Never promise a same-day refund — only Finance (VP Finance) can authorise expedited refunds.
